Latest University News

University Launches AI Campus Assistant

University of AI-Wich has officially launched its AI-powered Campus Assistant, a conversational chatbot available 24/7 to students, staff, and visitors. The assistant can answer questions about admissions deadlines, course schedules, campus facilities, and academic services — all in real time. Built on a locally-hosted language model, the system prioritises privacy by processing all queries on-campus without sending data to third-party cloud providers. Early pilot testing saw over 1,200 student interactions in the first week alone.

Student Feedback Shapes Digital Transformation

Following two semesters of surveys involving over 4,000 students and 800 campus visitors, the university overhauled its digital communication strategy. Students consistently reported frustration with slow email response times and fragmented information across departmental websites. In response, the university consolidated key services into a single AI-driven interface, reducing average query resolution time from 48 hours to under 30 seconds. A dedicated student advisory board continues to guide future development priorities.

AI Navigation Available for Visitors

First-time visitors and prospective students can now use the AI assistant as a live campus guide. Ask for walking directions to any lecture hall, cafeteria, sports facility, or administrative office and receive step-by-step instructions tailored to your current location. The assistant also provides opening hours, upcoming events, and accessibility information for each venue. Integration with the university's live event calendar means answers are always up to date, even during exam periods and open days.
